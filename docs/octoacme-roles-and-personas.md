# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors / Business Owners

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors or Business Owners provide organizational support and ensure a project remains aligned with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business need and expected value
- Secure or escalate funding, staffing, and organizational support
- Make or sponsor major scope, priority, and go/no-go decisions
- Resolve escalations that are beyond the delivery team's authority

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to align business outcomes and priorities
- Work with Project Managers on status, resource constraints, and escalations
- Consult Developers and other delivery leads when decisions affect feasibility or commitments

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide the technical approach and help the team deliver a reliable, maintainable solution.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and quality requirements
- Support engineering estimates and clarify implementation trade-offs
- Promote consistent engineering practices and review high-impact changes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, and technical reviews
- Advise Product Managers on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Coordinate with Project Managers on technical dependencies, risks, and schedule impacts

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers ensure that project decisions reflect user needs and support usable, accessible experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and validate assumptions
- Create and maintain user flows, prototypes, and design specifications
- Identify usability and accessibility requirements
- Gather feedback and test design concepts with users

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to shape problems, requirements, and acceptance criteria
- Partner with Developers to translate designs into implementable experiences
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to validate usability and accessibility against expectations
- Keep Project Managers informed about research or design dependencies that affect delivery

---

## Data and Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define how project outcomes will be measured and ensure the data needed for those measures is reliable.

### Responsibilities
- Define event instrumentation and data requirements
- Establish metric definitions, baselines, and reporting needs
- Validate the completeness and quality of collected data
- Analyze results and communicate evidence about project outcomes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect success metrics to product outcomes
- Work with Developers to implement instrumentation and data pipelines
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to verify tracking and reported values
- Share measurement plans and findings with Project Managers for status and stakeholder updates

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements
- Advise on threat modeling, data handling, and risk mitigations
- Determine when specialist reviews or approvals are required
- Track findings and communicate unresolved risks through the appropriate escalation path

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advise Technical Leads and Developers on secure design and implementation
- Coordinate with Product Managers on privacy or security trade-offs affecting scope
- Work with Project Managers to record risks, owners, mitigations, and escalation needs
- Partner with QA/Testing on relevant security and privacy verification

---

## Release and Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads coordinate production readiness and help ensure that services remain observable and supportable after deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness and deployment requirements
- Coordinate monitoring, rollback, and mitigation plans
- Plan operational handoffs and confirm support ownership
- Monitor releases and coordinate response to deployment issues

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to confirm release criteria and verification plans
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release timing, dependencies, and communications
- Consult Product Managers on launch impact and user-facing changes
- Partner with Security and Privacy Leads when release risks or controls require review

---

## Customer Support and Enablement Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Enablement Leads prepare customer-facing teams to support a change and bring customer feedback back to the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Identify support, training, and documentation needs
- Prepare support teams and customer-facing materials for launch
- Surface customer readiness concerns and anticipated support volume
- Gather and route post-release feedback and recurring issues

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Product Managers on customer impact, messaging, and feedback
- Work with Project Managers and Release and Operations Leads on launch readiness and communications
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify known issues and support troubleshooting

---

## Combining Roles
- On smaller projects, one person may cover multiple roles when appropriate.
- The team should still name an accountable owner for each responsibility and decision.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
